About EPIAIDEA

EPIAIDEA is a digital epidemiology and population analytics platform designed for high-stakes inference and real-world decisions. We integrate traditional epidemiologic theory with modern AI-enabled methods to translate digital traces into validated evidence.

Our Core Pillars

  • Epidemiology: Grounding data in established public health theory.
  • AI & Data: Scaling analytics with a focus on interpretability and bias awareness.

    • Evidence to Action

    Inference-ready outputs are the final stage of our pipeline. We don't just provide data; we provide validated evidence built for:

    • Policy Monitoring: Real-time tracking of health metrics and spatial-temporal epidemiology.
    • Access Metrics: Identifying county-level disparities using geospatial distance metrics and service listings.
    • Targeted Interventions: Guiding resources to where digital signals and structural care access are most mismatched.
