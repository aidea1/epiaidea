Common Questions

What types of projects do you take on?

I focus on work where rigorous epidemiologic methods meet AI-enabled analytics — surveillance platforms, access gap mapping, infodemiology pipelines, and policy dashboards with real-world decision impact.

Yes. I'm open to co-investigator roles, methods consulting, and collaborative grant submissions — particularly in digital epidemiology, health equity, and AI safety in clinical settings.

Absolutely — I've been featured in BBC News, Scientific American, Medscape, JMIR, and others. Reach out via email with your deadline and topic and I'll respond promptly.

Do you consult for health systems or government agencies?

Yes. I'm available for consulting engagements with public health departments, health systems, NGOs, and policy bodies on surveillance, analytics infrastructure, and evidence translation.
