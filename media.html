Clinical Leader Jan 29, 2026
Clinical AI Safety

Making AI Safety Routine: How Sites and Sponsors Can Continuously Monitor Clinical AI

Expert commentary on the systemic gap between one-time AI validation and the continuous monitoring infrastructure that clinical research sites and trial sponsors actually need. The piece addresses how AI tools drift after deployment — when patient populations shift, data pipelines change, or model inputs evolve — and why point-in-time validation is insufficient for tools embedded in ongoing clinical decision-making. Draws on pharmacovigilance frameworks and real-world AI deployment experience to propose an embedded safety monitoring approach analogous to post-market drug surveillance.

Injury Surveillance

Injuries from E-Bikes and E-Scooters Have Tripled

National emergency department data showing a threefold increase in e-scooter and e-bike injuries, with alcohol involvement and teenage riders identified as key risk concentrations. The underlying research used NEISS surveillance data to characterise the injury surge and its demographic distribution — findings that reached Scientific American, Medscape, U.S. News, and multiple international outlets within 48 hours of publication.
